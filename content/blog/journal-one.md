My name is Alex Bratek and I'm excited to work on this project! 

My group is working on a wave-survival co-op game where you have to protect an NPC with no survival instincts from waves of enemies. As waves are completed, the team chooses a downgrade to the NPC that lowers its chance of survival It's meant to be a comedic cooperative game that encourages player coordination and collaboration. However, we are planning to allow for a more intense and competitive play style as well, so that players can choose how they would like to experience the game.

Going into this semester, I was worried that I wouldn't resonate with or enjoy the game I would be helping create for the next year. This was due to the shake-up of our pre-production groups from the previous semester and resulting death of a game I was really excited to work on. Now, however, I am really looking forward to seeing how this new game comes together.
